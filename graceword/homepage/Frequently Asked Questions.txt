1. What’s the background story of GraceWord?
GraceWord was born from the journey of a rebellious second-generation Christian, gently found by God in a broken home. Growing up watching her Christian mother and father constantly argue, she felt distant from the “glory of God” and built walls of rebellion, refusing to draw near to Him. She thought God only existed in church—not in her tears, her fights, or her pain. But God never gave up on her. Time and again, He answered her prayers in real, tangible ways, showing her that He was not a distant God, but one who walked with her through hard days, listened to her cries, and held her hand when she was lost. Through these encounters, she slowly let go of her rebellion, learned to trust, and understood that grace is not something to take for granted—it is a gift to be seen, recorded, and cherished. Later, when she became a mother, she named her daughter Grace. Looking back on her journey, what she most wanted to pass on was not a perfect faith, but this truth: God’s grace is meant to be seen, recorded, and shared. That is how GraceWord was created—a tool to walk with God daily, draw strength from Him, and be renewed in faith. She hopes every Christian can leave their own “traces of grace” here: To write down God’s leading, so they never forget His kindness To journal struggles and weaknesses, finding strength in prayer To record life-changing moments, and pass this grace on to family—just as she wanted this gift to nourish her own parents and her daughter. GraceWord is not just an app; it is a calling: May every encounter with God become a grace to be seen, treasured, and shared.
